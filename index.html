Comprehensive Review and Methodological Advancement in Beta Diversity Measures

Case Western Reserve University

Advisor(s): Dr. Zihan Zhu

Sep 2024 - Sep 2025
Project Focus

Conducted a comprehensive review of dissimilarity measures and their mathematical properties, such as conditional negative definiteness, and evaluated their empirical performance across diverse real-world datasets.

Methodology

Systematically evaluated existing dissimilarity measures to determine which ones truly satisfy conditional negative definiteness. Employed PCoA, PERMANOVA, miRKat and rigorous simulation frameworks for thorough method validation.

Innovation

First systematically reviewed the mathematical properties of dissimilarity measures and evaluated their consequence in downstream analysis. Proposed remedial strategies for dissimilarity measures without ideal mathematical properties.

Outcome

Currently preparing a manuscript for submission to a peer-reviewed journal aimed at providing practitioners with methodological insights and enhanced tools for beta diversity analysis.

Review and Revamp of Microbiome Compositional Data Transformation

Case Western Reserve University

Advisor(s): Prof. Liangliang Zhang

Nov 2023 - Dec 2024
Overview

Conducted a systematic review on compositional data transformations focusing on strengths and limitations of existing techniques in microbiome data analysis.

Innovation

Developed a novel framework combining proportion conversion with contrast transformations, the CCT Framework, to enhance data handling in high zero-inflation microbiome datasets.

Methods Proposed

Proposed two new transformation methods, Centered Arcsine Contrast and Additive Arcsine Contrast, demonstrating improved performance in stabilizing variance and mitigating outlier influence.

Dual-Group Framework

Proposed a dual-group conversion framework for proportional data, specifically beneficial for two-sample testing, enhancing the power to detect differential abundance.
